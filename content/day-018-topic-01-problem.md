**Failure:** A learner assumes a project is covered by their personal trial, then works in a company project selected in the Console. The project is linked to a different billing account, so the intended trial credit does not cover its usage.

**Cause:** The sign-in account, active project and linked billing account were treated as one thing. A project switch changed the resource destination without changing the learner's mental model of who pays.

**Fix:** Stop creating resources. Recheck the active project ID and billing linkage with an authorized owner. Move the exercise to an approved sandbox or disposable project, then record its billing owner, allowed services and cleanup owner. Review any unexpected charges in Billing. A trial credit is a limited credit, not permission to use arbitrary projects.

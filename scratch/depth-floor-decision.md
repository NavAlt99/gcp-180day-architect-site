# User-approved depth-floor wording changes

All five live instruction files searched with case-insensitive patterns for concise, shorten, condense, proportional, efficient, trim, short, depth and scheduled time. Negative prohibitions on reduced depth remain intentional. Compact briefs/audit results and short technical terms describe structure or labels, not permission to shorten explanations. ONE_DAY_AT_A_TIME.md and RENDER_REVIEW.md contain no prose/time-depth reduction directive and continue referencing the canonical contract. No routine page-content reads or Phase B work.

## PAGE_AUTHORING_CONTRACT.md

Before:

> Scheduled time guides scope, not necessary depth;
> allow extra time for worked mechanisms.

After:

> 
> 
> Depth floor (user decision): conceptual depth may increase but must never decrease. Scheduled study time never caps or shortens an explanation. If a topic needs more worked mechanism, examples, or lab detail than the schedule suggests, write it and note the time overrun in the handoff; do not trim it. Token savings come only from structure (shared helpers, defaults, compact diagram specs, read budget), never from shorter prose, fewer subtopics, fewer examples, or thinner labs. Existing page content may be revised only to add or correct depth, not to condense it.
